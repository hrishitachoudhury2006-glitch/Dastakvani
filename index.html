Section 6(1) RTI Legal Escalation Engine

When citizen grievances remain unresolved past the 15/30 day threshold, Dastak Vani automatically compiles a formal, legally structured petition under Section 6(1) of the RTI Act 2005.

Sample RTI Application Draft: RTI-Act-2005-Sec6(1) 
BEFORE THE PUBLIC INFORMATION OFFICER (PIO)
Under Section 6(1) of the Right to Information Act, 2005

To:
The Public Information Officer (PIO),
Office of the District Supply Officer / Block Development Officer,
District: Muzaffarpur, State: Bihar.

Subject: Request for Information under Section 6(1) regarding Unresolved Citizen Grievance #DV-20260901-7F2A

1. Particulars of the Applicant:
   Name: Citizen Representative (Dastak Vani Civic Advocacy Network)
   Grievance ID: DV-20260901-7F2A
   Location: Saraiya, Muzaffarpur, Bihar

2. Statutory Inquiries Requested:
   (a) Daily progress report and action-taken log since complaint registration on 01/09/2026.
   (b) Certified copy of inquiry report submitted by the designated investigating officer.
   (c) Names and designations of officers accountable for the delay exceeding 15 days under Bihar Citizen Grievance Redressal Act.
   (d) Timeframe within which ration entitlement will be restored to the affected family.

3. Statutory Timeline:
   Information is required within 30 days as mandated under Section 7(1) of the RTI Act, 2005.

Fee: ₹10 (Indian Postal Order enclosed / BPL Exemption claimed).
